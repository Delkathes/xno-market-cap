# Absolute Scarcity Parity

> **Formula:** 1 unit of Asset A = (Total Supply of Asset B) / (Total Supply of Asset A)

The value of a single unit is inversely proportional to its total supply.

## Mathematical Proof

Using the formula: **1 Unit of A = (Total Supply of B) / (Total Supply of A)**

| Conversion       | Calculation                                          | Result       |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| 1 XNO → BTC      | 21,000,000 ÷ 133,248,297                             | ~0.1576 BTC  |
| 1 XNO → Gold     | 209,000,000 ÷ 133,248,297                            | ~1.568 KG    |
| 1 XNO → USD      | $20,800,000,000,000 ÷ 133,248,297                    | ~$156,080    |
| 1 XNO → EUR      | €16,000,000,000,000 ÷ 133,248,297                    | ~€120,061    |

---

## Key Takeaway

This table accurately reflects the **scarcity hierarchy**. Each XNO represents a much larger share of its fixed network than a single Dollar or Euro represents of their infinite and expanding networks.

> **Based purely on absolute, un-inflatable supply, a single unit of Nano is fundamentally scarcer than a single unit of any other asset on this list—including Bitcoin and Gold.**

This is the power of a fully distributed and absolutely fixed supply.
