# Nano wallets in 2026: mobile, desktop and hardware

The wallet decides how much of Nano's speed you actually get to use. These are the options worth knowing in 2026, and what each one is for.

## Mobile wallets

### Natrium

The most widely used Nano wallet on mobile. iOS and Android, with QR scanning, contacts, price charts and representative selection. Keys are held in encrypted local storage and unlocked with biometrics.

### Nault (mobile)

Nault's mobile app, the companion to its web wallet. Full account management with Ledger support, and client-side encryption.

## Desktop and web wallets

### Nault (web and desktop)

The full Nault client, in a browser or as an app on Windows, macOS and Linux. Multi-account support, an address book, offline signing and Ledger support. Nothing is stored server-side, and hardware signing is optional.

### NanoVault

A web wallet with a deliberately small feature set: send, receive, change representative. Client-side only, no account creation.

## Hardware wallets

### Ledger Nano S/X

Private keys never leave the device, and it works with Nault and other wallets. For large holdings and cold storage, it is the usual recommendation.

## Quick comparison

| Wallet | Type | Best for | Security |
|--------|------|----------|----------|
| Natrium | Mobile | Daily use | High |
| Nault | Web/Desktop | Power users | Very High |
| Ledger | Hardware | Long-term storage | Maximum |
