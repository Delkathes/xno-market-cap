# Understanding Nano: the technology behind instant transactions

Nano moves a transaction in under a second and charges nothing for it. Both properties come from the same design decision: there is no global chain to queue behind and no mining to pay for. Here is how the pieces fit together.

## The block-lattice architecture

Bitcoin records every transaction in one sequential chain. Nano does not. Each account keeps its own chain of blocks, and the design is called a **block-lattice**:

- Each account has its own chain, an account-chain.
- Only the account's owner can add to that chain.
- Transactions are asynchronous and independent of each other.

<Callout type="insight" title="No queue">Your transaction is processed immediately on its own chain, not behind thousands of others.</Callout>

## Open Representative Voting (ORV)

Nano reaches agreement with **Open Representative Voting** instead of energy-intensive mining:

1. **Delegation**: holders assign their voting weight to a representative.
2. **Voting**: representatives vote on whether a block is valid.
3. **Confirmation**: a block confirms once it reaches quorum, which is more than 50% of voting weight.

The whole sequence usually finishes in **under 1 second**.

## How it compares

| Feature | Nano | Bitcoin | Ethereum |
|---------|------|---------|----------|
| Transaction time | Sub-second | ~10 minutes | ~15 seconds |
| Transaction fee | $0.00 | $1-50+ | $1-100+ |
| Energy per transaction | 0.000112 kWh | 1,173 kWh | 62.56 kWh |

## What the design produces

Four properties:

- **Instant settlement**: no waiting for confirmations.
- **Zero fees**: $1 and $1 million cost the same to send, which is nothing.
- **Scalability**: the network handles thousands of transactions per second.
- **Low energy**: minimal environmental impact per transaction.
