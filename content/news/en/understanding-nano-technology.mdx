# Understanding Nano: The Technology Behind Instant Transactions

Have you ever wondered how Nano achieves instant, feeless transactions? In this article, we explore the innovative technology that sets Nano apart from traditional blockchain cryptocurrencies.

## The Block-Lattice Architecture

Unlike Bitcoin's single blockchain where all transactions are recorded sequentially, Nano uses a unique **block-lattice** structure. In this design:

- Each account has its own blockchain (account-chain)
- Only the account owner can modify their chain
- Transactions are asynchronous and independent

This means your transaction doesn't have to wait in line behind thousands of others. It's processed immediately on your own chain.

## Open Representative Voting (ORV)

Nano uses a consensus mechanism called **Open Representative Voting** instead of energy-intensive mining:

1. **Delegation** – Users delegate their voting weight to representatives
2. **Voting** – Representatives vote on transaction validity
3. **Confirmation** – Transactions are confirmed when they reach quorum (>50% voting weight)

The entire process typically completes in **under 1 second**.
