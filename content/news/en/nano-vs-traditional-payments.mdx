# Nano vs Traditional Payment Systems: A Comparison

When evaluating Nano as a payment method, it helps to compare it against the systems we use every day. Let's see how it measures up.

## Speed Comparison

| Payment Method | Settlement Time |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Nano | Under 1 second |
| Credit Card | 1-3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 1-5 business days |
| PayPal | Instant to 3 days |
| Wire Transfer | 1-2 business days |

## Fee Comparison

| Payment Method | Typical Fee |
|----------------|-------------|
| Nano | $0.00 |
| Credit Card | 1.5-3.5% |
| Bank Transfer | $0-25 |
| PayPal | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| Wire Transfer | $15-50 |

## Key Advantages of Nano

### For Consumers
- **No fees** – Keep 100% of your money
- **Instant** – No waiting for transactions to clear
- **Global** – Works the same anywhere in the world
- **Privacy** – No personal information required

### For Merchants
- **Zero chargebacks** – Transactions are irreversible
- **No middlemen** – Direct customer-to-merchant payments
- **No processing fees** – Increase profit margins
- **Instant settlement** – Improve cash flow

## Challenges to Consider

- **Volatility** – Price can fluctuate (though stablecoins exist)
- **Adoption** – Not yet accepted everywhere
- **Learning curve** – New technology requires education

## The Verdict

Nano excels in pure payment efficiency. For point-of-sale transactions, micropayments, and cross-border transfers, it offers clear advantages over traditional systems.
