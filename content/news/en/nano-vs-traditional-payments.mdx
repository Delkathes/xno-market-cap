# Nano vs traditional payment systems

Nano's pitch is speed and zero fees. The fair test is against the systems people already use, so here are the settlement times and the costs, side by side.

## Speed comparison

| Payment method | Settlement time |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Nano | Under 1 second |
| Credit card | 1-3 business days |
| Bank transfer | 1-5 business days |
| PayPal | Instant to 3 days |
| Wire transfer | 1-2 business days |

The card row is the one that gets misread: the tap at the terminal is instant, the settlement behind it is not.

## Fee comparison

| Payment method | Typical fee |
|----------------|-------------|
| Nano | $0.00 |
| Credit card | 1.5-3.5% |
| Bank transfer | $0-25 |
| PayPal | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| Wire transfer | $15-50 |

## Advantages for each side

### For consumers

- **No fees**: nothing is deducted in transit.
- **Instant**: no waiting for a payment to clear.
- **Global**: behaves the same anywhere in the world.
- **Privacy**: no personal information required.

### For merchants

- **No chargebacks**: transactions are irreversible.
- **No middlemen**: the customer pays the merchant directly.
- **No processing fees**: margins stay intact.
- **Instant settlement**: the money is usable immediately.

## Where it falls short

- **Volatility**: the price moves, which is why stablecoins exist.
- **Adoption**: it is not accepted everywhere yet.
- **Learning curve**: new technology, and users need to be taught.

## The verdict

<Callout type="insight" title="The verdict">Nano wins on pure payment efficiency: point of sale, micropayments and cross-border transfers. The advantage over cards and bank transfers is real and measurable. The adoption gap is the part that has not closed.</Callout>
