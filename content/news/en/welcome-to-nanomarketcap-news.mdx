# Welcome to NanoMarketCap News

Today we are launching the news section. It is the first part of the site that is not a number, and we are a little proud of that.

NanoMarketCap started with a simple idea: XNO is one of the most interesting assets in crypto, and its story is best told with data you can verify yourself. The live prices, the scarcity parity matrix, the supply calculators, the dominance charts. All of it is still here, and all of it is still free.

But data only gets you so far. Numbers tell you what is happening. They do not always tell you why. That is what this section is for.

## What you will find here

We are going to keep it simple. No aggregation, no noise, no clickbait. Three kinds of posts:

<Steps title="Three kinds of posts">
	<Step index="01" title="Platform updates">When NanoMarketCap changes, we write about it. The redesigns, the new tools, the reasoning behind them.</Step>
	<Step index="02" title="Nano ecosystem news">Developments across the network, community projects, tooling. The stuff we think actually matters.</Step>
	<Step index="03" title="Market notes">XNO price analysis with context. What is moving, why, and what it might mean.</Step>
</Steps>

## Why now

The short answer: the site is mature enough to deserve it. The parity tools and the calculators took most of our attention over the past months. Now that they are in good shape, we can finally spend time on the stories around the numbers.

The longer answer: Nano's fundamentals are easy to state and hard to believe.

<Callout type="insight" title="The short version">Zero fees. Settlement in under a second. A fixed supply that is fully distributed and cannot be inflated.</Callout>

We have put a lot of effort into making those claims checkable with our tools. The news section is where we explain what they actually mean.

## What is coming

The first real articles are already being written. A technical look at how Nano's consensus works in practice. A guide to reading our scarcity parity numbers without getting lost. A series on what XNO would need to reach different market caps, and what that would imply.

If you have a topic you want us to cover, or a project in the ecosystem that deserves attention, we want to hear from you. Reach out on our social channels, or use the partner form if you are building something on Nano.

Welcome to the news section. We will keep it honest, and we will keep it interesting.
