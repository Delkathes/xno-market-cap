# Nano ecosystem growth: Q1 2026 highlights

Integrations, community projects and the usual network numbers. Here is where the Nano ecosystem stood in the first quarter of 2026.

## New payment integrations

Several platforms added Nano support during the quarter:

- E-commerce plugins: new WooCommerce and Shopify integrations let merchants accept XNO.
- Point-of-sale: mobile POS systems now take instant Nano payments.
- Remittance: cross-border payment providers using Nano's zero-fee transfers.

## Community projects

### NanoGPT

An AI service that accepts Nano micropayments, and the clearest working example of what feeless transactions are good for.

### Nano tipper bots

Updated tipping bots on Reddit, Discord and X (Twitter) make it easier to send XNO to content creators.

### Educational resources

New tutorials, documentation and learning material in multiple languages, all of it lowering the barrier to entry.
