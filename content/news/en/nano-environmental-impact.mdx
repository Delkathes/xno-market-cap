# The Environmental Case for Nano

In an era of increasing environmental awareness, the energy consumption of cryptocurrencies has come under scrutiny. Here's why Nano stands out as an eco-friendly choice.

## The Problem with Proof-of-Work

Bitcoin and similar cryptocurrencies use Proof-of-Work (PoW) mining, which:

- Consumes more electricity than some countries
- Requires specialized hardware that becomes e-waste
- Creates competition to waste more energy

## Nano's Green Alternative

Nano uses Open Representative Voting (ORV) instead of mining:

- **No mining required** – Representatives simply vote
- **Minimal hardware** – Runs on standard servers
- **Fixed supply** – All Nano already exists, no new coins to mine

## Energy Comparison

| Network | Energy per Transaction |
|---------|------------------------|
| Nano | 0.000112 kWh |
| Ethereum | 62.56 kWh |
| Bitcoin | 1,173 kWh |

That means Bitcoin uses over **10 million times** more energy per transaction than Nano!

## Real-World Perspective

A single Nano transaction uses less energy than:

- Sending an email
- A Google search
- Charging your phone for 1 second

## Carbon Footprint

Studies have shown that the entire Nano network could run on the energy output of a single wind turbine, while still processing thousands of transactions per second.

## Sustainability Matters

Choosing Nano means:

- Supporting sustainable technology
- Reducing your digital carbon footprint
- Proving that crypto doesn't have to harm the planet
