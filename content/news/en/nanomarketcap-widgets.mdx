# NanoMarketCap Widgets: Live Nano Instruments for Any Website

Nano's live pulse now embeds anywhere on the web. [nanomarketcap.com/widgets](https://nanomarketcap.com/widgets) is live: a widget rack, a builder page where anyone assembles a live Nano instrument and drops it onto a website with one copy-paste. Seven instruments ship today: price, market cap, calculator, network stats, account balance, live transactions and donations, in all six site locales. No account required, and every number updates itself.

## What the rack holds

The builder is a configurator. Pick a widget on the left, tune the settings, watch the preview update, copy the snippet. The embed is a plain iframe plus a small hosted script (`widgets/embed.js`) that measures the widget's real height and resizes the frame to match. No fixed-height dead space, no clipping.

<Figure src="/news/nanomarketcap-widgets/01-builder-rack.png" alt="The NanoMarketCap widget rack builder with the seven instrument chips, a live preview and the embed code" caption="The rack: seven instruments, live preview, one copy-paste snippet" />

The seven instruments:

- **Price.** Live XNO price with 1H, 24H and 7D deltas, plus market cap, supply and rank.
- **Market Cap.** The same card with a hypothetical variant: Nano at Bitcoin's market cap.
- **Calculator.** An interactive XNO to fiat converter, preloaded with the live price.
- **Network stats.** Total blocks, cemented ratio, online weight and principal representatives.
- **Account balance.** A balance card for any `nano_` address.
- **Live transactions.** A WebSocket feed of network-wide confirmations as they happen.
- **Donate.** A QR donation card for your own address.

All seven speak all six languages (en, fr, de, es, it, nl). That matters more than it sounds: a widget embedded on a French blog shows French labels to that blog's visitors, with no extra work.

## The details that matter

**Currency hierarchy.** Each widget has a primary fiat currency (the big number), a strip of secondary fiats, and up to 8 crypto assets the value is also shown in. Stablecoins are excluded from the crypto picker on purpose: "1 XNO = 1.3 USDT" is noise, not information.

**No client-side API calls.** Prices, caps and supplies are injected at render time on the server, then cached. The widget makes zero API calls from the visitor's browser, so embeds work from any origin, on any site that accepts HTML, with no CORS headers to configure. The live feed is the exception: it needs a WebSocket connection from the browser, and it is the only part of the widget that does.

**Theme and width aware.** Widgets follow the visitor's theme, light or dark, and switch to a compact layout below 380px, so they behave on phones and in narrow sidebars. The embed script auto-fits the iframe height to whatever the compact or full height happens to be, so there is no wasted space.

**Attribution stays.** Every widget carries a permanent "Data by NanoMarketCap" plate. It is not removable, and that is deliberate: free live data, with the credit line attached.

## Who it is for

The obvious uses first: a community site putting the price in its header, an exchange listing page that wants live network stats next to the deposit instructions, a wallet provider embedding an account balance card into its dashboard.

Then there is the donation widget, which is quietly the most community-shaped instrument in the rack. Anyone running a project on Nano can generate a QR donation card for their own address in about a minute. No backend, no signup, no fee: it renders a QR code and deep links that any Nano wallet understands.

The rack exists to put live Nano data where Nano people already are, instead of making them come to us. Build yours at [nanomarketcap.com/widgets](https://nanomarketcap.com/widgets). It is free, it needs no account, and the donation cards are already being put to work by the community.
