# Introducing NanoBrowser

NanoBrowser is revolutionizing how we think about web browsing and digital currency. Instead of selling your attention to advertisers, NanoBrowser pays **you** in Nano for your time.

## How It Works

1. **Browse normally** – Use the web like you always do
2. **Earn XNO** – Receive micropayments for optional, privacy-respecting ads
3. **Instant payouts** – Withdraw your Nano anytime with zero fees

## Key Features

### Privacy First
NanoBrowser blocks trackers by default. Your browsing data never leaves your device.

### Built-in Wallet
A secure Nano wallet is integrated directly into the browser. No extensions needed.

### Cross-Platform
Available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.

- **Zero fees** – Every cent you earn is yours
